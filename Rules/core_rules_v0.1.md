# Nárëquenta Core Rules v0.1
Draft — 2025-10-25

## 1. What This Game Is
Nárëquenta is a TTRPG where heroes begin perfect and end spent.

Characters start at full strength in every aspect of themselves.
They do not gain levels.
They are remembered for how beautifully they burn out.

You are not trying to survive forever.
You are deciding *what parts of you are worth spending before you’re gone.*

---

## 2. Facets of the Self
Each character is defined by five Facets. Each Facet starts at 100%.

- VIGOR — body, endurance, force, instinct
- GRACE — movement, finesse, presence, seduction
- MIND — intellect, memory, structure, pattern
- SPIRIT — conviction, empathy, faith, meaning
- SHADOW — secrecy, intuition, dream, the unseen

Each Facet has:
- a **Current Value** (how much of that part of you is still usable right now)
- a **Maximum Value** (the highest it can be restored to)
- an **Efficiency** (how costly it is for you to act through that Facet)

At character creation:
- Current = 100
- Max = 100
- Efficiency = 1.0 (normal cost)

---

## 3. Acting in the World
When you do something meaningful, you choose which Facet you are using.

Examples:
- Swinging a blade to kill → VIGOR
- Dancing through arrows / seducing a guard → GRACE
- Solving runic logic / casting a structured spell → MIND
- Inspiring the broken / resisting despair → SPIRIT
- Vanishing, lying, seeing what should not be seen → SHADOW

### Action Cost
1. You **spend** some amount of that Facet’s Current Value (usually 5%–15%, depending on how intense the action is).
2. You automatically succeed. You are legendary until you’re not. The question is never “can I?” The question is “what will it cost me?”

When Current Value hits 0 in a Facet:
- You cannot act through that Facet anymore.
- You can still exist, but that part of you is essentially gone for now.

When ALL Facets hit 0:
- You are done. Dead, gone, ascended, faded, burned out, whatever fits the fiction of your ending.

---

## 4. Scarring / Refinement
After certain extreme actions (fights, miracles, revelations), you may **Refine** the Facet you used.

Refinement is optional, but powerful.

Refining does two things at once:
- Permanently LOWER that Facet’s Maximum Value (usually -1% to -3%)
- Permanently INCREASE that Facet’s Efficiency (future actions cost less Current Value)

In fiction: you become more precise, colder, more inevitable — but also more used-up, more scar tissue than flesh.

Example:
- Kai uses VIGOR in a brutal duel.
- She chooses to Refine VIGOR.
  - Her VIGOR Maximum drops from 100 to 97.
  - Her VIGOR Efficiency improves from 1.0 to 0.8 (costs 20% less to act with VIGOR going forward).

She is now more lethal per point spent, but can never be “100” again. Part of her youth is gone forever.

This is how characters “improve.”  
They don’t get bigger. They get sharper and smaller.

---

## 5. Rest and Recovery
When the scene / mission / operation ends, there is a Recovery Phase.

Recovery does:
- Restore some Current Value to each Facet you used (for example, you might get back 30% of what you spent),
- BUT only up to that Facet’s current Maximum.

Recovery does NOT:
- Raise Maximum Value,
- Undo Refinement,
- Make you young again.

You are always coming back a little less than you were.

---

## 6. GM Guidance: Rewarding Scars
At the end of each mission, the GM awards each character a pool of “Refinement Budget” (for example, 5% total to distribute).

The player can spend that Budget to Refine Facets they actually relied on during the mission.

This means:
- The world shapes you based on what you actually did.
- Training is not generic. It’s literally engraved into you.

The GM should also set the “Intensity Cost” of actions during play:
- Minor risk → 5% spend
- Serious risk → 10% spend
- Mythic, world-shaking, or desperate → 15%+ spend, plus automatic Refinement opportunity

---

## 7. Endgame
You don’t retire rich.
You retire hollowed and legendary.

By late play:
- Your Maximums are lower (60, 55, 43…)
- Your Efficiencies are inhuman (0.5, 0.4…)
- Every move costs almost nothing, but you have very few moves left.

When you burn your last Facet to zero, that’s the end of your story.
What happens to you — martyrdom, apotheosis, quiet disappearance — is part of the table’s shared fiction.

The campaign is the record of *how you chose to fade.*
