# 📜 Nárëquenta — Tales of the Waning

**Change Log**

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is inspired by Keep a Changelog, and adheres to Semantic Versioning when applicable.

---

## [v0.1] — 2025-10-25 (Private Development)

**Status:** Proof of Origin

- **Added:** Established the core mechanic of self-attrition: Actions consume Facet percentages1.
    
- **Added:** Defined the optional “Scarring” system which lowers maximum value in exchange for efficiency2.
    
- **Added:** Defined five Facets of the Self: VIGOR · GRACE · MIND · SPIRIT · SHADOW3.
    
- **Added:** Added Recovery Phase and Endgame rule4.
    

---

## [v0.2] — 2025-10-26 (Design Framework)

**Status:** Alpha Test Readiness

- **Changed:** Facet naming to **VITALIS · MOTUS · SENSUS · VERBUM · ANIMA**5.
    
- **Added:** Added the critical separation between **Maximum Essence ($\mathbf{E_{max}}$)** and **Current Essence ($\mathbf{E_{cur}}$)**6.
    
- **Added:** Added the **Waning Scale**, where low $\mathbf{E_{max}}$ grants **Efficiency ($\mathbf{A_{rate}}$)** bonuses7.
    
- **Added:** Added Hybrid Roll resolution ($\mathbf{E_P + E_S}$) and $\mathbf{E_{max}}$-mitigated Damage system8.
    
- **Added:** Added Multilingual support (PT-PT / EN-US) and sheet automation logic9.
    

---

## [v0.3] — 2025-10-26 (Attrition Scope Refinement)

**Status:** Alpha Test Readiness

- **Removed:** Removed Attrition cost ($\mathbf{E_{cur}}$) for most actions outside of combat (e.g., Social, Investigation)10.
    
- **Added:** Added Fixed Cost rule of **$\mathbf{2\%}$** for extreme stress situations outside of combat11.
    
- **Added:** Added **Symmetric Penalty ($\mathbf{10}$)** for NPCs, rewarding the player's narrative/tactical bonus in contested rolls12.
    

---

## [v0.4] — 2025-10-26 (Final Progress Loop)

**Status:** Alpha Test Ready

- **Removed:** Removed the **Decay Milestone Scarring** and 10% Reallocation rules (obsolete)13.
    
- **Changed:** Altered the rule for progression and decay to **The Waning Roll**14.
    
- **Changed:** Proficiency Choice rolls $\mathbf{4\text{d}6}$ (higher decay risk) in exchange for permanent **$\mathbf{2\text{d}10}$ Proficiency Dice ($\mathbf{D_{prof}}$)**15.
    
- **Fixed:** Fixed game title to **Nárëquenta — Tales of the Waning**16.
    

---

## [v0.5] — 2025-10-27 (Attack Rolls & Contested Combat)

**Status:** Alpha Test Ready - Core Combat Loop Defined

- **Changed:** The entire Action Resolution system now defaults to **Contested Rolls**17.
    
- **Added:** **Proficiency Tiers and Dice Mapping** that tie $\mathbf{E_{max}}$ decay to $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ acquisition18.
    
- **Changed:** **Waning Roll** is now the primary mechanism for converting $\mathbf{E_{max}}$ loss into $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$19.
    
- **Added:** Added **Critical Outcomes** system (Double Critical, Reversal) and **Proficiency Mitigation** ($\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ subtracts from $\mathbf{d100}$)20.
    
- **Added:** Added **Special Attacks** by trading $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$21.
    

---

## [v0.7] — 2025-11-14 (Precision Lethality & Tier-Neutral Balance)

**Status:** Alpha Test Ready - Core Ruleset Finalized

- **Changed:** Version increased from v0.5 to v0.7 due to the **complete restructuring of progression and combat**22.
    
- **Added:** **Initial Erosion ($\mathbf{1\text{d}10}$)** added for all Essences at character creation23.
    
- **Added:** **Maximum Limit ($\mathbf{50\%}$)** added for $\mathbf{E_{max}}$, defining the permanent success ceiling24.
    
- **Changed:** The **Proficiency Tier Table** was altered to **Uniform Progression ($\mathbf{1\text{d}10}$ per Tier)**, simplifying the scale and increasing power25.
    
- **Changed:** The role of $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ was **Unified** for: 1) Error Mitigation, 2) Attrition Reduction, and 3) **Additive Base Damage ($\mathbf{R_{prof}}$)**26.
    
- **Added:** Added the **Additive Damage Formula** (Precision Lethality): $\mathbf{D_{Final}} = \max \left( 0, (A_{FP} - \bar{M}_{Defense} + D_{Margin} + R_{prof}) \right)$27.
    
- **Added:** Added the **Defensive Tier Advantage Multiplier ($\mathbf{M_{DTA}}$)** to reduce damage from lower-Tier enemies ($\Delta T \ge 1$)28.
    
- **Added:** Added **Action Surge (AS) Progression**, linking the number of available Action Surges to the Proficiency Tier (up to 4 AS at Tier V)29.
    
- **Changed:** The **Attrition Cost** was mitigated and simplified to: $\mathbf{E_{cur} \text{ Loss}} = \max \left( 0, (7 - R_{prof}) \right)$30.
    
- **Added:** Added the rule of **Motor/Quality Essence Pairs** to determine which $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ pools are spent in combat31.
    

---

## **[v0.8] — 2025-11-15 (SPA Migration & Launcher Stabilization)**
**Status:** Alpha Test Ready - Architecture Stabilized
* **Added:** Implementation of the **Single Page Application (SPA)** architecture in `index.html`, unifiying the three sheets (`PC`, `NPC`, `GM_REF`) into a single page.
* **Added:** Full integration of the **Damage Calculator** into the GM Reference section (`index.html`).
* **Added:** All GM Reference headers and descriptions now support **Multilingual Localization** (`data-lang`).
* **Removed:** Obsolete HTML files (`pc_sheet.html`, `npc_sheet.html`, `gm_reference.html`).
* **Changed:** Sheet navigation was migrated from page *reload* to **internal JavaScript view transitions** (`showSheet()`), eliminating threading *glitches* and increasing application stability.
* **Fixed:** Corrected the calculation of the **root path** (*root\_dir*) in the Python *launcher* (`sheet_gui.py`) to resolve the fatal path duplication error when loading JSON localization files.
* **Fixed:** Corrected the path mapping logic and error handling of the `pywebview` API for the language selector, ensuring robust language initialization.

***

## **[v0.9] — 2025-11-17 (Decay Mitigation & Tier I Stabilization)**
**Status:** Beta Test Readiness
* **Changed:** Altered the **Waning Roll** decay rate. Universal Decay (Non-Focus) is now $\mathbf{1d6}$ (was $\mathbf{2d6}$) to slow the rate of permanent $\mathbf{E_{max}}$ loss.
* **Changed:** Altered the **Waning Roll** decay rate. Refinement Focus (Chosen Essence) is now $\mathbf{2d6}$ (was $\mathbf{4d6}$).
* **Added:** Added **Tier I Guarantee** at character creation: Player may set one Essence to $\mathbf{90\%}$ $E_{max}$ for instant Tier I status.
* **Fixed:** Corrected the **Action Surge (AS) Progression Table** maximum to **4 AS** at Tier V (was 5 AS in previous draft version).
* **Changed:** Updated all core files and documentation to reflect the **v0.9** version.

## 💡 Core Philosophy: Attrition is Meaning

Nárëquenta is a TTRPG about **Beautiful Erosion**. Heroes start at their peak and gradually fade as they act and spend their Essence. Progression is the **defining of character through loss**.

**Core Axiom (v0.8):** Power is a finite resource. The real threat is the **Extinction of Essence**. **Proficiency Compensates for Decline**, allowing maximum impact with minimal capacity.

## ⚙️ System Summary (v0.8)

The game is built around Essence management, **Level-Neutral Damage**, and **Level-Based Mitigation**.

  * **E\_max (Maximum):** Has a **Hard Floor of 50%**. The reduction determines your **Proficiency Tier**.
  * **E\_cur (Current):** The usable energy. Defines the success threshold ($\mathbf{d100 \le E_{cur}}$).
  * **Damage:** Calculated using the **Additive Damage Formula** that privileges proficiency.
    $$\\mathbf{D\_{Final}} = \\max \\left( 0, (A\_{FP} - \\bar{M}*{Defense} + D*{Margin} + R\_{prof}) \\right) \\times M\_{DTA} \\text{}$$

  * **$\mathbf{M_{DTA}}$ Multiplier:** The **Complete $\mathbf{M_{DTA}}$ Grid** was added and restructured in v0.8 to explicitly manage damage advantage/reduction based on the Tier difference.
  * **Architecture:** The sheet application was migrated to a **Single Page Application (SPA)**, unifying the PC, NPC, and GM references into a single `index.html` file.

# Nárëquenta: Alpha Test Scenario — The Rescue of the Last Echo

**Test Focus (v0.7):** Testing **Tier I+ Attrition Control** and the lethality of the **Tier-Neutral Damage Formula**.
**Duration:** 2-3 Hours.

## 1. Starting Point: The Waning Sanctuary

The PCs are guardians of a **Waning Sanctuary** where the Echoes of legends are preserved. One such Echo is under attack by **The Silence** (an entity that feeds on unrealized potential). The Silence causes an **Anxiety Debuff** (All Attacker rolls have $\mathbf{-5}$ applied to $\mathbf{R_{prof}}$ for damage calculation only) and the **Waning Echo** has 5 turns until it fades completely.

* **Preparation:** Ensure all PCs have rolled **Initial Erosion ($\mathbf{1\text{d}10}$)**. All $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ are at $90-99\%$.

## 2. Phase 1: The Prodigal Crossing (Testing Motor/Quality Pairs)

* **Goal:** Force the spending of $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ across multiple Essences.
* **Challenge:** A Barricade of Mistic Force blocks the way.
* **Action:** PCs must spend effort to bypass the challenge (requires 3 quick successes).

| Action | Motor ($\mathbf{E_{P}}$) | Quality ($\mathbf{E_{S}}$) | Attrition (Per Success) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brute Force** | VITALIS | MOTUS | $\max(0, 7 - R_{prof}) + 1\%$ |
| **Quick Climb** | MOTUS | VITALIS | $\max(0, 7 - R_{prof}) + 1\%$ |

* **Success Check:** The PC must feel the pressure of the $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ drop from the cost of action and the Anxiety Debuff.

## 3. Phase 2: The Enigma of Sacrifice (Testing High-Risk Attrition)

* **Goal:** Test VERBUM / ANIMA usage and the vulnerability of a low $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ pool.
* **Challenge:** The Echo's door is sealed by a Mystical Enigma requiring **Focus and Will**.
* **Action:** The PC must:
    * **Solve:** $\text{VERBUM}$ (Motor) + $\text{SENSUS}$ (Quality).
    * **Break:** $\text{ANIMA}$ (Motor) + $\text{VERBUM}$ (Quality).
* **Success Check:** Requires a successful roll ($\mathbf{d100' \le E_{cur}}$). Failure should result in the **$\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ Motor being penalized by $\mathbf{1\text{d}6}\%$** (simulating extreme mental strain) in addition to the standard cost.

## 4. Phase 3: The Final Act and Progression Setup

* **Goal:** Test the **Tier-Neutral Damage Formula** and the need for **Action Surges**.
* **Challenge:** A **Form Guardian** (NPC, Tier I, $\mathbf{E_{cur}} \approx 90\%$, $\mathbf{HP 100}$) materializes.
* **Special Rule:** The PC with the lowest $\mathbf{E_{max}}$ (highest Tier) should be encouraged to attack first to test the efficiency of their $\mathbf{A_{FP}}$.
* **Damage Rule:** Use the **Defensive Tier Advantage ($\mathbf{M_{DTA}}$)** when the PC is hit (e.g., PC Tier III vs NPC Tier I $\rightarrow \times 0.75$ Multiplier).

| Rule Tested | PC Tier IV $\rightarrow$ Tier I NPC | Formula Component |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Offense Power** | PC's high $\mathbf{R_{prof}}$ yields high $\mathbf{A_{FP}}$. | $100 - d100'$ |
| **Defense Balance** | PC takes reduced damage. | $\times M_{DTA}$ |

* **Exit:** The GM guides the PCs to a moment of **Long Rest** and prompts the **Waning Roll** at the end of the chapter: "Which Essence betrayed you? What are you willing to lose to master it?"
