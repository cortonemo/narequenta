# Nárëquenta: Alpha Test Scenario — The Rescue of the Last Echo

**Test Focus:** Forcing Essence use to trigger the first **Scarring ($\mathbf{E_{max}} < 85\%$)** and testing **Damage Mitigation**.
**Duration:** 2-3 Hours.

## 1. Starting Point: The Waning Sanctuary

The PCs are guardians of a **Waning Sanctuary** where the Echoes of legends are preserved. One such Echo is under attack by **The Silence** (an entity that feeds on unrealized potential). The Silence causes an **Anxiety Debuff** (+1% Attrition cost to all actions) and the **Waning Echo** has 5 turns until it fades completely.

## 2. Phase 1: The Prodigal Crossing

* **Goal:** Spend $\text{Vitalis} / \text{Motus}$ quickly in the *Pristine* phase ($\mathbf{A_{rate}} = 30\%$).
* **Challenge:** A Barricade of Mistic Force blocks the way.
* **Action:** PCs must:
    * **Brute Force:** $\text{Vitalis}$ (Primary) + $\text{Anima}$ (Secondary) - $\mathbf{A_{rate}} \approx 32\%$.
    * **Quick Climb:** $\text{Motus}$ (Primary) + $\text{Sensus}$ (Secondary) - $\mathbf{A_{rate}} \approx 32\%$.
* **Success Check:** Requires 3 to 4 quick successes. The PC must feel their $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ drop from $\mathbf{100\%}$ to $\mathbf{80\%}$ or less.

## 3. Phase 2: The Enigma of Sacrifice

* **Goal:** Test $\text{Verbum} / \text{Anima}$ usage with the high $\mathbf{A_{rate}}$ ($40\%$).
* **Challenge:** The Echo's door is sealed by a Mystical Enigma requiring **Focus and Will**.
* **Action:** The PC must:
    * **Solve:** $\text{Verbum}$ (Primary) + $\text{Sensus}$ (Secondary).
    * **Break:** $\text{Anima}$ (Primary) + $\text{Verbum}$ (Secondary).
* **Success Check:** Requires a Verbum roll with high $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$. Failure should highlight the cost of unrefined potential.

## 4. Phase 3: The Final Act and Scarring

* **Goal:** Test full Hybrid Combat, $\mathbf{D_{conv}}$ and Damage Mitigation.
* **Challenge:** A **Form Guardian** (NPC with $100 \text{ HP}$) materializes.
* **Special Rule:** The PC with the lowest $\mathbf{E_{max}}$ (who has already scarred to $\le 84\%$) should be encouraged to attack to test their **$\mathbf{D_{conv}}$** (1d6 to subtract from the cost).
* **Damage:** The Guardian uses a weapon that deals $10 \text{ Base Damage}$.
* **Exit:** The GM guides the PCs to a moment of **Long Rest** and asks: "Which Essence betrayed you? What are you willing to lose to master it?"
