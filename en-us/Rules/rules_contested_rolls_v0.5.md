# ⚔️ Nárëquenta: Contested Rolls & Proficiency

This document details the v0.5 mechanics for Action Resolution, Proficiencies, and Damage in Nárëquenta.

## 1. Proficiency Tiers and Dice Mapping

The **2d10 Proficiency Dice ($\mathbf{D_{prof}}$)** gained from the Waning Roll (Core Rules §4) are converted into a **Proficiency Tier** based on the character's current $\mathbf{E_{max}}$ of the Refined Essence.

| Proficiency Range (Emax) | Proficiency Dice ($\mathbf{D_{prof}}$) | Die Type | Average (Rounded Up) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 96% – 100% | None | — | — |
| 80% – 95% | 1 die | $\mathbf{1\text{d}6}$ | 4 |
| 60% – 79% | 2 dice | $\mathbf{2\text{d}6}$ | 7 |
| 50% – 59% | 3 dice | $\mathbf{3\text{d}6}$ | 11 |
| <50% | 3 or 4 dice | $\mathbf{3\text{d}10}$ or $\mathbf{4\text{d}10}$ | 17 / 22 |

## 2. Using Proficiency Dice

$\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ have two primary uses: Error Mitigation and Attrition Reduction.

### A. Error Mitigation (Contested Rolls)

When rolling to hit ($\mathbf{d100 \le \mathbf{E_{cur}}}$), the player rolls their $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ and **subtracts the result from the $\mathbf{d100}$ result**. This is the primary way to turn a marginal failure into a success or avoid a critical fail.

### B. Attrition Reduction (Energy Cost)

The average result of the $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ used (see table above) is **subtracted from the total $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ loss** (Attrition Cost). This represents the increased efficiency gained through permanent sacrifice.

### C. Special Attacks (Trading Dice)

Players may temporarily **sacrifice** $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ from their pool to perform enhanced actions, such as **Precision Attacks** (harder to land, higher damage) or to enhance narrative impact. Characters without Proficiency cannot perform these enhanced actions.

## 3. Energy Depletion from Damage Roll

When a character takes damage, the amount of $\mathbf{E_{cur}}$ lost is capped by the maximum possible result of the highest $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ die available to them.

| Proficiency Range (Emax) | Max Energy Depletion Cap from Damage |
| :--- | :--- |
| 96% – 100% | — (No cap, as no proficiency) |
| 80% – 95% | Up to 6 (max of $\mathbf{1\text{d}6}$) |
| 60% – 79% | Up to 6 (max of $\mathbf{2\text{d}6}$) |
| 50% – 59% | Up to 6 (max of $\mathbf{3\text{d}6}$) |
| <50% | Up to 10 (max of $\mathbf{3\text{d}10}$ or $\mathbf{4\text{d}10}$) |

This cap ensures that wear-and-tear pacing is respected, and even high damage output does not disproportionately empty a character's core Essence.
