# Nárëquenta: Rules for Rituals and Renewal (Recovery)

Rituals close the game cycle (Spend → Fade → **Renew**), allowing the PC to manage their daily fatigue ($\mathbf{E_{cur}}$) and accept their permanent loss ($\mathbf{E_{max}}$).

## 1. Focus Renewal (Recovery - E_cur)

This ritual allows the PC to recover their mental and physical focus, returning to their daily potential.

- **Trigger:** Long Rest (e.g., a safe night's sleep).
- **Process:**
    1. The **Current Value ($\mathbf{E_{cur}}$)** of all Essences returns to their current **Maximum Value ($\mathbf{E_{max}}$)**.
    2. The **Action Surge (AS) Pool** is **restored** to its total determined by the Tier (Section 5 of the Core Rules).
- **Narrative Cost:** Renewal has no additional mechanical cost; the cost is the time and the risk of interruption during the Long Rest.

**Note on Permanent Loss:** Attrition is only temporary ($\mathbf{E_{cur}}$). The system **DOES NOT** restore the **$\mathbf{E_{max}}$** that was permanently lost.
