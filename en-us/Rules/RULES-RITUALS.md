# Nárëquenta: Rituals and Renewal Rules

Rituals close the loop of the game (Spend → Fade → **Renew**), allowing the PC to manage their daily fatigue (E_cur) and accept their permanent loss (E_max).

## 1. Focus Renewal (Recovery - E_cur)

This ritual allows the PC to recover their focus and physical energy, resetting to their intrinsic potential.

- **Trigger:** Long Rest (e.g., a night of safe sleep) or Short Ritual (e.g., one hour of meditation).
- **Process:** The **E_cur** of all Essences returns to their current **E_max** value.
- **Narrative Cost (Alpha Provisional):**
    - **Rest:** The PC must sacrifice something narrative (e.g., they must narrate a disturbing dream that costs -1d4% of their Verbum E_cur at the start of the next day).
    - **Ritual:** The PC must spend a **Rare Resource** (e.g., a dose of "Memory Bloom," a finite consumable) or accept a **Short-Term Debuff** (e.g., -10 to the next Sensus roll).

## 2. Scarring Ritual (Reallocation - E_max)

This is the moment to accept permanent loss and redefine the self, gaining efficiency.

- **Trigger:** Reaching a **Decay Milestone** (e.g., E_max drops to 84%, 69%, etc.).
- **Process:** Move up to **10% E_max** (total) from one Essence (donor) to another (receiver).
- **Narrative Cost:** The PC must narrate the memory, sacrifice, or moment of acceptance that justifies this permanent loss. This act must be dramatic and meaningful.
    - *Example:* "I accept my body will never be as strong (Vitalis -10% E_max), but that pain forged my will (Anima +10% E_max)."
