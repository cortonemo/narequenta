## 💡 Core Philosophy: Attrition is Meaning

Nárëquenta is a TTRPG about **Beautiful Erosion**. Heroes start at their absolute peak (100% potential) and gradually fade as they act and spend their Essence. Progression is not about number growth, but about **defining character through loss**.

**Axiom:** Power is a finite resource. The real risk is not death, but the **Extinction of Essence**.

### Core Ideas

* You begin at **100%** in every Facet of self.
* When you act, you **spend** from that Facet ($\mathbf{E_{cur}}$).
* You may choose to **Refine** that Facet: permanently lower its maximum ($\mathbf{E_{max}}$), but gain **Proficiency Dice ($\mathbf{D_{prof}}$)** for efficiency.
* Rest restores what remains of you, but never what you’ve already burned away.

## ⚙️ System Summary (v0.5)

The game is built around Essence management and **Contested Rolls**.
- **E_max (Maximum):** Permanently decreased by the **Waning Roll (2d6/4d6)**. This reduction determines your **Proficiency Tier**.
- **E_cur (Current):** The daily usable energy. Defines the success threshold ($\mathbf{d100 \le E_{cur}}$).
- **Contested Rolls:** Actions are resolved by both Attacker and Defender rolling. The Attacker uses $\mathbf{D_{prof}}$ to **Mitigate Error** (subtracting from the d100 result).
- **Progress:** Triggered by the **Waning Roll** at the end of each chapter/mission. The decay risk grants **Proficiency Dice ($\mathbf{D_{prof}}$)** for high-efficiency actions.
