III. Damage Formula ($\mathbf{D_{Final}}$)

$$\mathbf{D_{Final}} = \max \left( 0, (A_{FP} - \bar{M}_{Defense} + D_{Margin} + R_{prof}) \right) \times M_{DTA}$$

Where:

$\mathbf{A_{FP}}$ (Attacker Full Potential): $\mathbf{100 - (d100 - R_{prof})}$.

$\mathbf{D_{Margin}}$ (Defender Margin): $\mathbf{d100_{D} - E_{cur}}$.
