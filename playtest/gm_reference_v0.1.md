# GM Reference v0.1

SESSION FLOW
1. Briefing / Setup: why this mission matters.
2. Play scenes. Any meaningful action costs Facet %.
   - Player chooses Facet.
   - You set cost: 5 / 10 / 15+.
   - They reduce CURRENT by that much × their Efficiency.
3. If an action is mythic or near-death, offer Refinement:
   - Player may lower that Facet’s MAX by 1–3 permanently.
   - In exchange, that Facet’s Efficiency improves (future actions cost less).
4. When mission ends, go to Recovery Phase.

RECOVERY PHASE
- Restore some CURRENT in the Facets that were used (ex: regain 30% of what was spent).
- Never go above current MAX.
- Give each player a Refinement Budget (ex: 5% total).
  - They can apply that 5% as permanent MAX loss + Efficiency gain in any Facet that was actually used this mission.
  - They decide how they’re changing.

PACING / TONE
- Players should feel powerful and costly, not weak and bullied.
- Never say “you fail.” Say: “You can absolutely do that. It will cost you 10% SPIRIT.”  
  Let them decide what they’re willing to burn.

DEATH / END
- When all Facets hit CURRENT 0 → they’re done.
- Treat the ending as sacred. Ask them: how are you remembered?
